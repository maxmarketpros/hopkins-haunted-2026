Concessions staff play a vital role in creating a positive event atmosphere. These jobs involve selling food and drinks, managing inventory, and providing excellent customer service. For those who enjoy working with people but prefer a less performance-heavy role, concessions offer a great alternative.

Typical duties include:

- Operating cash registers and handling transactions.
- Preparing and serving snacks and beverages.
- Keeping concession areas clean and organized.
- Assisting guests with questions or special requests.
